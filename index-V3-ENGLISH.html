03 — Technology

Small on the outside. Ambitious on the inside.

NutriSense is envisioned as a HealthTech concept that combines biosensors, connectivity, and data visualization.

Hardware

Biosensors

A conceptual architecture based on sensors and microfluidics to explore the possibility of obtaining biometric information through a wearable device.

Software

Digital platform

A conceptual web interface displays indicators, trends, and statuses clearly from any device.

Connectivity

Connected ecosystem

The concept envisions connectivity with other digital services to build an integrated technology experience.
